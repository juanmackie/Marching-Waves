Generation modes

Each mode produces a distinct artistic effect from the same source image.

Marching Contours

Uses the Eikonal Equation to generate elevation-style contour lines. Ideal for topographical maps, flowing forms, and line art.

Flow Streamlines

Follows the gradient flow of the image. Creates stream-like patterns that trace the direction of edges and features.

Weighted Stipple

Density-based stippling: more dots in darker areas. Creates a hand-stippled, pointillist effect.

TSP Art (Continuous Line)

Joins all contour segments into a single continuous path, like a traveling salesman tour. One unbroken line.

Cross Hatch

Overlapping sets of parallel lines at controlled angles. Density maps to image brightness for a sketch-like appearance.

Frequently asked questions

What is Marching Waves?

A free browser-based tool that converts images into contour-based artwork using Marching Squares and the Eikonal Equation. Upload any image and choose from six distinct artistic styles.

Is it free? Do I need to sign up?

Completely free. No registration, no installation, no data uploads to servers. Everything runs locally in your browser.

What image formats are supported?

Any image file your browser can display — JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, and more. Drag and drop or click to select.

Can I export my results?

Yes. Export as SVG for vector editing in design tools like Illustrator or Inkscape.

Does it work on mobile?

The interface is responsive and works on mobile and tablet browsers. Processing performance depends on device capabilities.

What is the Eikonal Equation?

It's a partial differential equation that models wavefront propagation. Marching Waves uses it to solve distance maps on images, creating smooth contour paths that follow image features.
